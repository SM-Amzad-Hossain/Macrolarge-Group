Welcome to Macrolarge Group

Macrolarge Group brings together two specialist businesses built around a single idea: the way a building runs and the impact it has on the planet shouldn't be handled by two disconnected teams.

Our portfolio includes Macrolarge Facilities Management and Macrolarge Eco Sustainability Consultancy. One keeps your premises running safely, cleanly, and efficiently day to day. The other helps you reduce your environmental footprint, meet sustainability targets, and future-proof your operations against changing regulation.

Most facilities providers stop at maintenance. Most sustainability consultants never set foot on site. We do both, which means our advice is grounded in how your building actually operates, and our facilities work is shaped by where the industry is heading, not just where it has been.

Whether you need a single service or a joined-up strategy across both, Macrolarge Group gives you one point of contact, one team that understands your site, and one relationship instead of several.
